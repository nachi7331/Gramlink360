Accountable Governance

How Gramlink 360 Solves Your Village Issues

From reporting to photo-verified resolution—every step is tracked transparently with zero intermediaries.

01

Submit in 60 Seconds

Pick your department (Water, Electricity, Sanitation), select your ward, write the issue or snap a photo. No paperwork required.

02

Instant Token & SLA Timer

Receive a unique 10-digit tracking token (e.g. GL-2026-XXXX). The system initiates an official 48-hour SLA countdown timer.

03

Officer Action & Photo Proof

The assigned field officer (Gram Sevak or Lineman) repairs the issue on ground and uploads resolution photo proof to the public portal.
